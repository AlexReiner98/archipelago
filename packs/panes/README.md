# Panes

The page's one canvas: the pane it is drawn in, at the page's address. A root
pack: its code changes only through a root re-pin, never by the refiner.

The tabs went on 18 Nov 2026 (decision e_mnuthcap): what a person sees is the
islands they have on, and an agent's show adds its island to the canvas and
lights the item in the island side bar, never moving the view. The chats at
work are the side bar's Sessions part, and the count of those waiting on you
stands beside the islands button.

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| `page/panes.js` | Draws the canvas at the page's address, with back and forward in the band |
| `page/pane-address.js` | What an address of the page draws in the pane |
