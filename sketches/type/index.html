A visual identity grown from real archipelagos

Archipelago's look is generated, not drawn: patterns of geometry, texture and palette grown from the ecologies of real archipelagos, each one a point on a few clean parametric spectrums. Many places, one system.

Where the ridges meet the brand system

The ridges already share the app's paper, ink and "sea is blue" idea, so they can be the brand's pictures without new colour tokens. They are not yet a mark: nothing works below about 200 px, nothing is drawn on a light ground.
